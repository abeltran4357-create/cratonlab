Sedimentary rock

Mudrocks: shale, mudstone, siltstone, claystone

At least 75% silt- and clay-sized grains (under 63 µm). Shale is finely laminated and splits into sheets; mudstone mixes silt and clay without shale's layering; siltstone is mostly silt; claystone is mostly clay.

How it forms (petrogenesis)

Shale is deposited from very slow-moving water: lakes, lagoons, river deltas, floodplains and offshore below wave base; most shales are marine. Clay flocculates quickly in salty seawater. Burial compacts the mud and aligns the clay flakes into parallel layers, which gives shale its fissility. Black shale (over 1% carbonaceous material) forms in anoxic, stagnant water.
