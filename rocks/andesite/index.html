Igneous rock

Andesite

Intermediate, extrusive and fine-grained, often grey.

How it forms (petrogenesis)

Andesite is characteristic of convergent plate margins and is the dominant rock of island arcs. Water released from the subducting slab lowers the melting point of the mantle wedge, producing basaltic melt. That basalt evolves toward andesite by fractional crystallization (olivine and amphibole crystallize and are removed first), by mixing with evolved rhyolitic magma in crustal chambers, and by melting and assimilating crustal rock.

Source: Wikipedia: Andesite

Equivalent: Diorite is its intrusive equivalent.
