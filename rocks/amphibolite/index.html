Metamorphic rock

Amphibolite

High-grade rock from mafic igneous rock such as basalt, made of amphibole (hornblende) and plagioclase with aligned crystals.

How it forms (petrogenesis)

Amphibolite usually forms by metamorphism of mafic igneous rock such as basalt or gabbro; impure carbonate sediments can also yield it, especially near granite intrusions. Amphibolite-facies conditions are above about 500 °C and below 1.2 GPa, reached by continued burial and heating beyond greenschist facies.
