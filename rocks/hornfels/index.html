Metamorphic rock

Hornfels

Product of contact metamorphism of fine-grained rocks such as mudstone or volcanic rock; non-foliated, with randomly oriented crystals (micas, pyroxene, amphibole).

How it forms (petrogenesis)

Hornfels forms by contact metamorphism: rock baked and recrystallized in the solid state by the heat of an intrusion, without deformation, most often in the aureole around granite in the upper or middle crust. The hornfels facies is the lowest-pressure metamorphic facies, with minerals such as cordierite, andalusite and sillimanite.
