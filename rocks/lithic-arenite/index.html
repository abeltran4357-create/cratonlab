Lithic arenite

An arenite with over 10% rock fragments, and more rock fragments than feldspar.

How it forms (petrogenesis)

Lithic arenites are texturally immature to submature. They form where large volumes of unstable rock fragments are produced, and they are associated with river conglomerates and other fluvial deposits. Forearc basins tend to accumulate sand rich in lithic grains and plagioclase.
