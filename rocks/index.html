Rock types

How each common rock is defined and how it forms (petrogenesis), with photos and thin-section links. Thin sections often settle what a hand sample can't: the minerals, textures and fossils inside.

Igneous

Named by composition (felsic to ultramafic) and grain size (intrusive and coarse, or extrusive and fine).

GraniteDioriteGabbroPeridotite and duniteRhyoliteAndesiteBasaltKomatiiteObsidian, pumice and scoria

Sedimentary

Clastic rocks are named by grain size and composition; chemical, biochemical and organic rocks by what they're made of.

Metamorphic

Foliated rocks get coarser from slate to gneiss as grade increases; non-foliated rocks are named by mineralogy.
