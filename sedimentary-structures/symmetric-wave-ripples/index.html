Symmetric (wave) ripples

Ripples with both sides of similar slope, often with sharp crests and rounded troughs.

How it forms
Two-way, oscillating flow such as beach swash and backwash or wave action in shallow water.
Typical setting
Beaches and other shallow water with wave action
Way up
Crests are sharp and point up; troughs are rounded.
Flow direction
No. Not used for current direction.
