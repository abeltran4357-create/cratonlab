Groove casts and tool marks

Long straight ridges or marks on the base of a bed.

How it forms
Objects dragged by a current cut grooves into soft mud, which are then filled by the overlying sand.
Typical setting
Sand rapidly deposited on mud, common in turbidites
Way up
They occur on the bottom of beds.
Flow direction
Gives the flow axis (the line of flow, not which way along it).
