Slump folds

Tight folds in a sediment layer bounded by undeformed beds.

How it forms
Sediment on a slope fails and moves downslope under gravity.
Typical setting
Sloping depositional surfaces
Way up
Not effective as way-up indicators and easily confused with tectonic folds.
Flow direction
Can indicate paleoslope direction once way-up is known from other structures.
