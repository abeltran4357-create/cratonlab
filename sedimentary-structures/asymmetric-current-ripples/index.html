Asymmetric (current) ripples

Small ripples with a gentle upstream (stoss) side and a steeper downstream (lee) side.

How it forms
One-way currents, such as rivers or wind, in the lower flow regime.
Typical setting
Rivers and other one-way currents
Way up
Ripple troughs are rounded and crests are sharp or truncated.
Flow direction
Yes. The steep side faces downcurrent.
