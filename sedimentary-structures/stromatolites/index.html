Stromatolites

Layered, often domal sedimentary structures built by microbial mats.

How it forms
Photosynthetic microbes such as cyanobacteria trap and bind sediment in stacked sheets, growing upward toward light.
Typical setting
Shallow water; modern examples mainly in hypersaline lakes and marine lagoons, such as Shark Bay, Western Australia
Way up
Not a way-up indicator
Flow direction
No.
