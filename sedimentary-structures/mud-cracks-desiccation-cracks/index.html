Mud cracks (desiccation cracks)

Polygonal cracks on a bedding surface; in cross section, V-shaped cracks filled by the overlying sediment.

How it forms
Wet mud shrinks and cracks as it dries in the air.
Typical setting
Floodplains, playa lakes, tidal flats: anywhere mud is exposed
Way up
Cracks narrow downward to a point (V shape) and are filled from above. Crack edges curl upward.
Flow direction
No.
