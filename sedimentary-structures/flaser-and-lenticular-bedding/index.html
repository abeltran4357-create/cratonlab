Flaser and lenticular bedding

Flaser bedding is mostly sand ripples with thin mud drapes. Lenticular bedding is the mud-dominated counterpart, with isolated sand lenses in mud.

How it forms
Intermittent flow: ripples form during active flow and mud settles during quieter periods.
Typical setting
Mainly tidal environments; less often rivers
Way up
Not a primary way-up indicator.
Flow direction
Ripple cross-lamination within it can show flow direction.
