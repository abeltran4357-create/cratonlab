Geopetal structures

A cavity, such as inside a shell, partly filled with sediment at the bottom and with cement (for example calcite) above.

How it forms
Sediment settles to the floor of the cavity under gravity, and cement later fills the remaining space.
Typical setting
Fossiliferous limestones; vesicles in lavas can behave similarly
Way up
The sediment fill marks the bottom, and its surface records the original horizontal and later tilt.
Flow direction
No.
