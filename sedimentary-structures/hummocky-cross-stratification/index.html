Hummocky cross-stratification

Gently undulating laminae forming low mounds (hummocks) and hollows (swales) that cut across one another, with long wavelengths (about 1–5 m) and low heights.

How it forms
Large storms, through combined one-way and oscillatory flow.
Typical setting
Shelf below fair-weather wave base and above storm wave base
Way up
Not commonly used.
Flow direction
No. The mounds have no preferred orientation.
