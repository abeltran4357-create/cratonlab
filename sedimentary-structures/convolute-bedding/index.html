Convolute bedding

Laminae deformed into chaotic folds within an otherwise undeformed bed.

How it forms
Loosely packed sand settles and escaping water fluidizes it, deforming the laminations.
Typical setting
Rapidly deposited, water-saturated sand
Way up
Only if the folds are truncated by younger, undeformed laminations.
Flow direction
No.
