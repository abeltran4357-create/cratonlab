Convert strike and dip notation

Convert a plane between right-hand-rule azimuth, dip direction/dip and quadrant notation, and get its pole.

Open the strike and dip converter

What it converts

  • Right-hand-rule strike/dip (e.g. 220/35)
  • Dip direction/dip (e.g. 310/35)
  • Quadrant bearings (e.g. N40E, 35NW)
  • The pole to the plane as plunge→trend

Under the right-hand rule the dip direction is 90° clockwise from the recorded strike.
