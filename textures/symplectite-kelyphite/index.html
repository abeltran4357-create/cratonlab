Metamorphic texture

Symplectite / kelyphite texture

Fine intergrowths of two or more minerals. Kelyphite is a symplectite rim formed by garnet breakdown.

What it means

Breakdown reactions as pressure or temperature changed.

Where to look for it

Granulite, eclogite, garnet peridotite
