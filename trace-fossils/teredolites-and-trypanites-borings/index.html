Teredolites and Trypanites (borings)

Borings rather than burrows: Teredolites bore into wood or peat (woodgrounds), and Trypanites bore into hard rock or shell (hardgrounds).

Behavior
Bioerosion
Likely maker
Boring bivalves and other borers; the textbook illustrates piddock (Pholas) and angelwing bivalve borings.
Setting
Name-bearers of the Teredolites (woodground) and Trypanites (hardground) ichnofacies.
