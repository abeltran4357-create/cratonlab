Paleodictyon

A honeycomb-like network of hexagonal or polygonal tunnels; mesh elements are typically 1–3 cm, and whole patterns can cover up to a square metre. Some have vertical tubes to the sea floor.

Behavior
Farming (probable)
Likely maker
Uncertain. Proposals include a farming or food-trapping burrow, a xenophyophore cast, and others; empty modern examples have been found near Mid-Atlantic Ridge vents at about 3,500 m depth.
Age range
Precambrian/Early Cambrian to the present.
Setting
Deep sea.
