Quartz in thin section

Recognized by low relief, low birefringence (about 0.009, 1st-order gray to white), and no cleavage or twinning. Uniaxial positive. Plastically strained grains show sweeping (undulose) extinction.

Telling it apart

Plagioclase is biaxial, has cleavage and is usually twinned. Orthoclase looks similar but has negative relief. Cordierite alters readily to yellowish pinite and is often twinned.

Where you'll find it

Granite, rhyolite, sandstone, quartzite, schist, gneiss
