Cyclosilicate · Uniaxial (−)

Tourmaline in thin section

Strongly pleochroic and darker when its long dimension is at right angles to the lower polarizer, the opposite of biotite. No cleavage. Medium relief. Stubby to needle-like euhedral prisms.

Plane-polarized light

ColorBrown, Green, Blue / lavender
Pleochroism (color change on rotation)Strong
ReliefModerate
CleavageNone
ShapePrismatic, Needles / fibrous, Euhedral

Cross-polarized light

Maximum birefringenceabout 0.025
Highest interference color2nd-order blues, greens, 3rd-order
ExtinctionParallel
TwinningNone

Interference colors are often masked by the mineral's own color.

Telling it apart

Biotite has a perfect cleavage and is darker when its cleavage is parallel to the polarizer. Colored apatite is also darker when parallel.

Where you'll find it

Granite, pegmatite, schist, detrital grains in sandstone
