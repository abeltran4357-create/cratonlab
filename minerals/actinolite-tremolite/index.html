Actinolite / tremolite in thin section

Colorless (tremolite) to pale or deep green (actinolite), with pleochroism that strengthens with iron content. Amphibole cleavages at 56°/124°. Inclined extinction about 10–21° in elongate sections. Colors up to upper 1st or low 2nd order.

Plane-polarized light

ColorColorless, Green
Pleochroism (color change on rotation)None, Weak, Strong
ReliefModerate, High
CleavageTwo at 56° / 124°
ShapeNeedles / fibrous, Prismatic

Cross-polarized light

Maximum birefringenceabout 0.022
Highest interference color1st-order yellow–red, 2nd-order blues, greens
ExtinctionInclined, Parallel
TwinningNone, Simple (two halves), Polysynthetic stripes

Telling it apart

Hornblende has higher indices and a larger extinction angle. Colorless richterite can be hard to tell from tremolite.

Where you'll find it

Greenschist, metamorphosed mafic rocks, marble (tremolite)
